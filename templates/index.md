## Editor-confirmed templates

### JMVA LaTeX template

Please use the **JMVA LaTeX template** when preparing your manuscript for the
*Journal of Multivariate Analysis*. You can browse the source files on GitHub
or download the latest template as a ZIP archive:

- [Go to the template repository](https://github.com/jmva-home/JMVA-templates)
- [Download the latest template (ZIP)](https://github.com/jmva-home/JMVA-templates/archive/refs/heads/main.zip)

### Getting started

1. Download and extract the ZIP archive.
2. Open `JMVA_template.tex` and replace the sample title, author names,
   affiliations, addresses, and contact email with your own details.
3. Keep `ref.bib` alongside the LaTeX file and replace the sample references
   with those for your manuscript.

Follow the guidance in the template and consult the journal's
[Guide for Authors]({{ site.author_guide_url }}) when preparing your submission.
