## Announcements

### Early Career Advisory Board (ECAB) – Call for Applications!

*Journal of Multivariate Analysis* (JMVA) is pleased to announce a call for
applications for its Early Career Advisory Board (ECAB). The ECAB is intended
to recognise outstanding early career researchers in multivariate analysis and
related areas, provide them with meaningful exposure to editorial work, and
support the continued development and visibility of the journal.

**Application deadline: 31 January 2027.**
