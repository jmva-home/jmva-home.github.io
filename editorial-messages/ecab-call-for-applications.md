## Early Career Advisory Board (ECAB) – Call for Applications!

*Journal of Multivariate Analysis* (JMVA) is pleased to announce a call for
applications for its Early Career Advisory Board (ECAB). The ECAB is intended
to recognise outstanding early career researchers in multivariate analysis and
related areas, provide them with meaningful exposure to editorial work, and
support the continued development and visibility of the journal.

> **Application deadline: 31 January 2027.**

[View the publisher's announcement ↗](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-multivariate-analysis/about/announcements/early-career-advisory-board-ecab-call-for-applications)

### Eligibility

We welcome applications from researchers who meet the following requirements:

- Have completed their doctoral degree.
- Are within five years of their first independent research career in a
  tenure-track or equivalent position.
- Have an active research profile aligned with the aims and scope of JMVA.
- Have a genuine interest in supporting rigorous peer review and scholarly
  publishing.

### Role and responsibilities

ECAB members will contribute to JMVA's editorial community by:

- Conducting reviews for **2–3 submissions to JMVA annually**.
- Submitting **at least one manuscript to JMVA during the two-year term**
  (one submission every two years). These manuscripts will be processed in the
  same way as regular submissions.
- Acting as ambassadors for JMVA, for example on social media and at conferences.

### Appointment

Appointments to the ECAB will be for a **two-year term**. ECAB members who make
exceptional contributions may be considered for future appointment as JMVA
Associate Editors, subject to the journal's editorial needs and standard
appointment process.

### How to apply

Applications should be submitted as a **single PDF file** including:

- The applicant's name and affiliation, with a link to a personal or
  institutional webpage.
- Areas of expertise.
- Reviewing experience.
- A list of publications.

Applicants may also include a brief statement outlining their motivation for
joining the ECAB and how they hope to contribute to the journal.

Please send applications by email to both JMVA editors:

- **Dietrich von Rosen:** [Dietrich.von.Rosen@liu.se](mailto:Dietrich.von.Rosen@liu.se)
- **Tõnu Kollo:** [Tonu.Kollo@ut.ee](mailto:Tonu.Kollo@ut.ee)

Use the subject line: **JMVA ECAB Application – [Applicant Name]**.

Applications will be accepted until **31 January 2027**.

### Selection

All applications will be reviewed by the JMVA editors, and applicants will be
informed of the decision **within four weeks after the deadline**. The names of
selected ECAB members will be posted on the
[JMVA Editorial Board webpage](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-multivariate-analysis/about/editorial-board).

JMVA is committed to building a diverse and internationally representative
editorial community. We encourage applications from qualified early career
researchers across all regions and backgrounds whose expertise aligns with the
journal's scope.
